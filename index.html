Product

Built for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, dental, auto repair and any business where a missed call is a lost customer.

📞

AI Receptionist

Answers in two rings, around the clock, in a natural voice trained on your business. Qualifies the caller, answers common questions, books directly into your calendar and sends the confirmation — no hold music, no voicemail.

🚀

Outbound Lead Engine

Speed‑to‑lead: every new web form or ad lead gets a call back within 60 seconds, while they're still on your site. Lead reactivation: we call your old quotes and past customers and turn dead lists into booked appointments.

How it works

Live in days, not months.

We handle the setup. You keep your phone number, your calendar and your CRM.

We learn your business

A short onboarding call: your services, service area, pricing rules, hours and how you want calls handled and escalated.

We connect your tools

Forward your existing number, connect your calendar and CRM. Leads flow in from your website, Google Ads and Meta forms automatically.

Your agent goes live

Every call answered, every lead called back, every appointment booked and logged. You get a daily summary and full call recordings.

Why it matters

The math is simple.

Service businesses miss roughly a quarter of their inbound calls, and most of those callers phone the next company on the list. Calling a new lead back within a minute makes you far more likely to reach them than waiting an hour.
